Every algorithm on these pages is written in the standard notation. A letter names a layer; on its own it means a quarter turn clockwise as you look at that face; a prime means anticlockwise; a 2 a half turn. Press Show on any row to watch the cube do it from solved, then do the same on yours and compare. The letters on the centres name positions, not colours: F is always the face towards you.

Solved. Pick a move on the right.
Two conventions to know. A wide turn is written both ways in the wild: Rw and r mean the same thing, and both are accepted wherever these pages ask you to type moves. Slice directions are the ones the speedcubing community uses (M like L, E like D, S like F); some older books define them the other way round, which is worth knowing if an algorithm from a book refuses to work.
