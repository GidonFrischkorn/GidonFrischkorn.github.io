Method and algorithms

Everyone shares this page, nobody shares progress. Each solver's checkmarks are stored in the browser they use, so two people on two devices stay separate — but two people on the same browser see the same profile list, and clearing your browser data erases progress. There are no passwords here and nothing is private.

Your data is yours to take. The JSON export restores exactly, in this browser or another one. The CSV is one row per event, ready for read_csv() with nothing to unpack first.
