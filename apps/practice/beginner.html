Beginner method

layer by layer, step by step
Programme Notation Beginner Last layer

Seven stages solve any cube: the white cross, the white corners, the middle layer, then the yellow cross, the yellow edges, the yellow corners into place, and the yellow corners turned. Each stage below has a way to recognise what you are looking at and a worked example. Pick an example, hold your own cube the same way, and step through the moves one at a time with →. The letters on the cube name the faces the moves refer to. If you get lost, the notation page shows every move on its own.

Where this comes from. The stages and algorithms follow J Perm's beginner guide at jperm.net/3x3, which is the one to watch if you want it explained on video. Every example here is computed by the same engine that checks the CFOP programme, so the pictures are what the moves really do.
